# The Forty Thousand

**Maria** adjusts her shirt collar with a hand that is still trembling. Try as she might, the sound of the door slamming violently continues to echo in her mind. Throughout the day, the image of her husband repeatedly comes to mind—surrounded by bottles and cigarettes, shouting threats. "If you go to that convention, you'll see what happens when you get back!"

![](https://i.ibb.co/FbJjnV3m/1000132853.png)

For a brief moment, her eyes fix on a young woman sitting in front of her, secretly envying her peace and serenity. But she does not know that this young woman, **Filipa**, at 28 years old, also carries immense anxiety in her heart. Year after year, she waits for a look of interest to rescue her from solitude. But that look never comes. Another year is passing, and she remains alone, with no one to share her life with.

Nearby, in the same grandstand, a couple intertwines their hands during the song. Seeing them so united, Filipa laments deep down: "I wanted so much to experience that happiness...". But Filipa does not know that **José and Francisca**, married for 25 years, are facing their worst nightmare. Their teenage son, raised with such affection, has become rebellious and directionless; they ask themselves where they failed as parents.

After the song, everyone sits back down. Then, Francisca and José notice an impeccable young boy, focused on taking notes. "If only our son were like that...", they think. But that boy, **João**, is fighting his own battle. Every feminine perfume floating in the air and every elegant silhouette trigger the memory of his schoolmate who whispers bold provocations to him daily. Determined to resist, João longs to enter the working world, certain that adult life will be much easier.

Young João looks at **Pedro**, 45, standing at the top of the grandstand wearing his Attendant vest. The youth thinks Pedro's stability would be his salvation, never imagining that the family man's head is throbbing—crushed by his boss's shouting, the frantic ringing of phones, and suffocating deadlines at the company where he works. How will he free himself from so much pressure when he still has over twenty years left until retirement?

Beside him, however, is **César**, retired for a year, who carries the weight of a gray spot in his chest. While listening to such edifying talks, he cannot prevent his mind from being invaded, from time to time, by the word *tumor*, coldness itself, spoken during a recent medical appointment. It is a reminder that time slips away like fine sand through his fingers. Silently, he wonders if he will live long enough to attend more conventions like this one.

At that precise moment, a song and a prayer mark the end of those three days of immense comfort—a refreshing convention that helped all those brothers and sisters distance themselves, as much as possible, from their personal dramas. Unexpectedly, an additional song begins to play. Then another. And another...

In unison, forty thousand voices rise in thrilling enthusiasm, transforming the stands of the Estádio do Dragão into a place of pure unity where no one, despite their secret battles, is truly alone.

![](https://i.ibb.co/m5j1VvNz/1000132858.jpg)

Maria forgets her husband's threats. Filipa breaks free from the suffocating shadow of loneliness. José and Francisca reignite their hope for their son. João pushes the temptations tormenting him from his mind. Pedro stops hearing the frantic ringing of phones. And César? He knows time is slipping through his fingers, but he also understands that time only fully runs out for those who have already lost their faith.

In that sublime moment, the structure of the stadium seems to shake. Forty thousand voices, merged into a single breath, raise an immense, clear song that vibrates and resonates against the metal and concrete.

Even wounded by life's battles, they sing an undeniable truth in unison:

"Feeling fear is human.   
In my own strength, I’m frail.   
You have been my rock and refuge;   
Your power never fails.   
Help me be courageous,   
And let my heart be brave.   
Lasting harm can never hold me​—   
No prison, gate, or grave."   

It is the triumph of joy over grief. An immense faith that does not bow to pain. In that moment, forty thousand souls catch their breath and find a strength they didn't know they had!

Returning home, they carry the absolute certainty that wherever hope lives, neither fear, nor loneliness, nor death itself has the final word.
